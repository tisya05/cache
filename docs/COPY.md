## Prove — idle
- Title: `Prove your progress`
- Subtitle: `Math checks it. You get tokens. Nobody sees your data.`
- Left column: `Stays private` / Income, Transactions, Merchants, Spend breakdown, Everything / footer `Only you`
- Right column: `Goes public` / `Your tier` / the tier number / footer `That's it`

The tier shown under "Goes public" is the tier being **claimed**, not a result.
